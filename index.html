A developer who builds both the interface and the intelligence behind it.

I'm Sumit Kumar, a developer focused on two things that increasingly belong together: clean, fast web experiences and the AI systems that make them feel smart. Most of my work sits at that intersection — a landing page that converts, a dashboard that responds, an assistant that actually understands the question.
